Performance and component analysis

The restructured product page, measured

The restructure set out to put what buyers decide on, the details, dimensions and price, straight under the buy box, and to drop the sections that cost scroll without helping. This report measures whether visitors now use that content more, and whether more of them add to cart and buy. Cabinets moved to the new layout on 25 September; sideboards, media units and chests of drawers on 5 October.
